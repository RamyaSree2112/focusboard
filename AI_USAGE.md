AI assistance was used for:

- Getting guidance on component structure and reusable components.

- Mobile: 390 × 1128
- Tablet: 768 × 1024
- Desktop: 1440 × 1000

All major features were manually checked, including task creation, editing, deletion, searching, filtering, and carousel navigation.
